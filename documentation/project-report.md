# KAKUMA CONNECT PROJECT REPORT

**Project Theme:** Local Experience & Professional Networks

**SDGs:** 8, 10 and 17

**Prepared by:** Rhoda Idriss Kodi

## 1. Introduction

Kakuma Connect is a digital platform designed to connect local entrepreneurs, skilled individuals, freelancers and customers in the Kakuma community.

The project aims to promote local businesses, create opportunities for employment and strengthen professional networks through digital technology.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many people in Kakuma have skills, products and services but face challenges reaching customers and finding employment opportunities.

There is a need for a digital platform where local businesses can promote their products and skilled individuals can showcase their services.

## 3. Project Objectives

- To promote local products and businesses.
- To connect freelancers with potential customers.
- To create digital employment opportunities.
- To improve access to local products and services.
- To encourage networking and partnerships.

## 4. Target Users

- Local entrepreneurs and small business owners.
- Freelancers and skilled individuals.
- Customers and community members.
- Young people seeking employment opportunities.

## 5. Website Features

Kakuma Connect includes:

- Homepage introducing the platform.
- Product section displaying local products.
- Product search and category filtering.
- Shopping cart and checkout form.
- Freelancer registration form.
- Contact form for communication.

## 6. Technologies Used

- HTML: Structures the website.
- CSS: Designs and styles the website.
- JavaScript: Adds interactive features such as searching, filtering and shopping cart functions.

## 7. Contribution to Sustainable Development Goals

**SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth:** Supports local businesses, entrepreneurship and employment opportunities.

**SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities:** Creates a platform where people can promote their skills and access opportunities.

**SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals:** Encourages collaboration between entrepreneurs, freelancers, customers and organizations.

## 8. Testing

The website was tested in Google Chrome.

The following features were tested successfully:
- Website navigation.
- Product display.
- Product search.
- Shopping cart.
- Removing products.
- Checkout form.
- Freelancer registration.
- Contact form.

## 9. Future Improvements

- Connect the website to a MySQL database.
- Add secure user registration and login.
- Store product and customer information.
- Add real payment integration.
- Allow users to upload product images.
- Improve mobile accessibility.

## 10. Conclusion

Kakuma Connect is a digital solution designed to promote local experience, entrepreneurship and professional networking in Kakuma.

The project demonstrates how web technologies can help connect people, support businesses and create opportunities for economic growth and inclusion.
